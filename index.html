Sectional Run

VFR cross-country practice · fictional chart

Fly N421AP from Cedar Valley to Harbor Point. The plane holds 110 knots on its own, so you only steer and pick altitudes. Nothing stalls and nothing crashes. The challenge is reading the chart: stay legal around the airspace, avoid towers, and answer checkride questions as you reach each chart feature.

  • ◀ ▶ turn (hold) · ▲ ▼ change target altitude by 500 ft
  • RADIO call approach or a tower before entering their airspace
  • 3D tilts the chart to show the airspace as stacked slabs
  • Tap anything on the map to find out what it is · pinch or − + to zoom · drag to look around
  • Keyboard: ← → turn, ↑ ↓ altitude, R radio, V 3D, X speed, P pause, L land

The airspace, airports, towers and place names are invented for practice. This is not a real chart, so never use it for navigation.
